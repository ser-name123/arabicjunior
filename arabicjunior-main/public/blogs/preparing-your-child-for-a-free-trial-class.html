How Parents in the UAE Can Help

  • Create a Positive Outlook: Let your child know it’s fun—not a test.
  • Introduce Basic Arabic: Use simple phrases like “Marhaban” (Hello) before class.
  • Prepare the Setup: Ensure a quiet room, stable internet, and a charged device for online sessions.
  • Stay Nearby, Not Distracting: Sit close, but allow the instructor to guide.
  • Communicate with Tutors: Share your child’s strengths and any learning preferences.
