Introducing Arabic at an early age boosts memory, focus, and overall school readiness. Grade 1 is the ideal time for children to absorb language naturally, especially when lessons are engaging and age-appropriate. Parental Support: Your Role in the Journey

Parents are our partners in success. For Grade 1 learners, even a few minutes of Arabic exposure at home can make a huge difference. We encourage parents to:

  • Use Arabic greetings at home like “Salam” or “Shukran”
  • Celebrate small wins (like learning the alphabet)
  • Stay involved by reviewing vocabulary and class notes
  • Join feedback sessions with tutors to track progress
